# Roadmap

This document outlines the planned improvements and additions to the DevOps & Data Engineering Knowledge Base.

## 🎯 Vision

To be the most comprehensive, practical, and community-driven knowledge base for modern DevOps, Data Engineering, and AI/ML practices.

**Core Principles:**

- Production-ready examples
- Real-world best practices
- Clear, actionable guidance
- Continuous updates
- Community-driven
